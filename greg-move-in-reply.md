**Reply to Greg — Move-In Binder idea**

Hi Greg,

Great question — and yes, this is a really natural fit for what we've been building. The platform is essentially a checklist-and-accountability engine already (role-based logins, evidence/photo uploads, secure storage, email notifications, and CSV/Excel/PDF exports), so a move-in version would reuse a lot of what's there rather than starting from scratch.

The move-in binder is a slightly different shape than our community inspections, which is actually what makes it valuable as its own module:

- It's tracked **per resident / per move-in**, not per community.
- It runs in **phases over time** (prep → move-in day → welcome home → follow-ups) with **different department directors** owning different items.
- Items aren't pass/fail — they're "completed," with a date, the initials of who did it, and an optional signed form attached.
- It can enforce **compliance gates** — for example, blocking "ready for physical move-in" until the POC/1823 is approved and the medication form is signed.

The payoff is exactly the blind-spot visibility you're describing: a live dashboard per resident showing what's done, what's still pending, who owns it, and any compliance item that's holding things up as the move-in date approaches.

I'd suggest we start with a focused first version — an editable move-in template, a per-resident checklist the directors complete, and a progress board — then layer in the compliance gates and reminders once the basics are working. If you can share the exact phases and items from your current binder, we can map them in directly and pilot it with one community.

Happy to walk you through a quick mockup whenever you'd like.
